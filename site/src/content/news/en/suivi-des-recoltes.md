---
title: "Tracking harvests and water used"
description: "Every weighed harvest gives the yield per hectare and the water used per kilo."
date: 2026-10-27
---
A new dashboard page records each harvest: crop, plot, weight.

The system then computes the **yield per hectare** and the **water used per kilo harvested**. This is the measure that lets us compare plots and check that irrigation is well dosed.
