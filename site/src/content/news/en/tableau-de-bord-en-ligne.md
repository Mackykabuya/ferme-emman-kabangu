---
title: "The farm dashboard is online"
description: "One screen to follow every plot: moisture, soil state, irrigation and alerts."
date: 2026-10-20
---
The farm dashboard is now available online, from a computer or a phone.

It brings together on a single screen:

- the moisture and state of each plot;
- irrigation recommendations;
- a log of every action on the valves;
- alerts sent by e-mail.

For now the sensors are simulated: installing real sensors is the next step.
